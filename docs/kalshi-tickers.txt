Kalshi series tickers: crypto 15-minute and daily temperature high/low
=========================================================================

Fetched from Kalshi's public API on 2026-10-08 (only series with open markets that day).
Kalshi adds, renames and retires series; a title can even be wrong (KXHIGHTSDF is titled
"SATX" but settles on Louisville). ALWAYS read the market's rules before trading it:

  curl -s "https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?series_ticker=KXHIGHNY&status=open&limit=1" \
    | python3 -c "import sys,json; m=json.load(sys.stdin)['markets'][0]; print(m['rules_primary'])"

Use the series ticker as `series:` in a portfolio strategy. Event tickers (one market
window) look like SERIES-YYMONDD (daily) or SERIES-YYMONDDHHMM (15-minute, close time in
US Eastern). Individual markets add a strike suffix, e.g. KXHIGHNY-26OCT09-T76.


CRYPTO, 15-MINUTE UP/DOWN
-------------------------
Every 15 minutes, 24/7. YES = the price at close (60-second average of the CF Benchmarks
Real Time Index) is at or above the price at open. One market per window. The target price
is in the market's floor_strike once the window opens. Settlement: CF Benchmarks.
Spot proxy feed: coinbase_spot (product e.g. BTC-USD).

  series            title (Kalshi)              coinbase product
  KXBNB15M          BNB 15 Minute               BNB-USD
  KXBTC15M          Bitcoin price up down       BTC-USD
  KXCRYPTOLEAD15M   Coin Race 15 minutes        n/a (multi-coin, custom strike)
  KXDOGE15M         Dogecoin 15 Minute          DOGE-USD
  KXETH15M          ETH 15M price up down       ETH-USD
  KXHYPE15M         Hype 15 min                 HYPE-USD
  KXNEAR15M         NEAR 15m                    NEAR-USD
  KXSOL15M          Solana 15 minutes           SOL-USD
  KXXRP15M          XRP 15 Minute               XRP-USD
  KXZEC15M          Zcash 15min                 ZEC-USD

  All nine Coinbase products answered on 2026-10-08; Coinbase spot is a proxy for the
  CF Benchmarks index, not the settlement value.
  KXCRYPTOLEAD15M ("Coin Race") compares coins and uses a custom strike, which the
  feed_agrees gate can't evaluate.


DAILY TEMPERATURE (HIGH / LOW)
------------------------------
One event per day with several bracket markets ("greater", "less" or "between" a strike, in
degrees F). Settlement: the value The Weather Company reports for the station named in the
rules (weather.com/kalshi), NOT the NWS climate report, for every series below.
Rules read like "If the minimum temperature recorded at New York City (CLINYC) for Oct 9,
2026, is greater than 57F according to The Weather Company, then Yes." Read them for each
series: the station and the date matter, and they differ by series.

Forecast feed: nws_forecast with the station's lat/lon (a free NWS forecast for the same
spot, a predictor, not the settlement source). Coordinates below come from
api.weather.gov/stations/<ICAO>, with the ICAO code inferred from the station code in the
rules (CLINYC -> KNYC).
